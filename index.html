Workplace Effectiveness Diagnostic

Find out where you stand before you start training.

A short, scenario-based diagnostic that measures how you'd actually handle real workplace situations — not just whether you know the definitions. Your result becomes the starting point of your personal development plan.

~40minutes, one sitting
32scenario & readiness items
Instantresult on completion
  • 1 Situational judgement — 22 realistic workplace scenarios, choose the most effective response.
  • 2 Self-reflection — 15 short statements about how you generally work.
  • 3 Digital & remote-work readiness — 10 quick practical questions.
There are no trick questions and no perfect score expected — this is diagnostic, not a pass/fail exam. Answer as you honestly would at work, not as you think sounds best. Once started, a timer keeps the whole assessment to one sitting; you'll always see time remaining and a warning before it runs out.

Need more time due to a disability or because this isn't your first language? Request an accommodation before you begin — extended time and alternate formats are available on request.

Accommodations

We want your result to reflect your actual workplace judgement, not how well this format suits you. If any of the following apply, contact us at contact@avecgazelle.com.ng before you start, and we'll arrange it:

  • · Extended time (no penalty — just let us know roughly how much more you'd like)
  • · This isn't your first or strongest language
  • · You'd prefer questions read aloud or in a larger text size
  • · A screen-reader-compatible or audio version
  • · Any other access need not listed here — just ask
